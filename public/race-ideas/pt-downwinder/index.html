PT Downwinder: Salvation at the breakwater

Wait for a big winter southerly, launch at Port Hadlock, and run the length of Port Townsend Bay to Boat Haven.

Who
Singlehanded dinghies and windsurfers
When
Winter, on short notice — whichever day brings a strong southerly
Launch
Port Hadlock boat ramp
Start
Line between a temporary mark and a committee on the beach
Finish
Either a line between a boat and the race mark outside Boat Haven, or the fuel dock inside the breakwater
Safety
Raccoon as safety boat; possibly Salish Rescue too

Why

A southerly blows straight up the bay, so one long run is all downwind — a chance to plane on purpose for a few miles, then finish at home.

Logistics to work out

  • Getting boats there: ferry boats down to Hadlock before the start.
  • Getting dollies back: bring dollies back up to Boat Haven so boats can be hauled at the finish.
  • Finish choice: the outside line keeps the finish in open water; the fuel dock is sheltered and easy to land at.
  • Safety cover: confirm Raccoon and reach out to Salish Rescue.
  • Calling the day: agree on a wind range and how much notice racers get.
