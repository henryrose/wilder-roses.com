Text to Sail

A text-message list that helps Port Townsend small-boat sailors get on the water together. A sailor texts when they're heading out; subscribers get one short text and can reply to join.

To subscribe, text SAIL to

(877) 360-6751

We'll ask what name other sailors should see, then confirm your subscription.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help at any time.

How it works

1. Subscribe

Text the number above. Any message starts signup (SAIL is easy to remember). You'll be asked for a display name and then get a confirmation text. You can also sign up through the club's Discord channel.

2. Hear about sails

When a subscriber posts an outing ("going out 5:30 tomorrow, room for 1"), everyone else gets one text with the time, place, open spots and the wind and tide forecast.

3. Reply to join

Reply with the code in the text (for example J1) to join. You'll only hear more about that sail if it changes or is cancelled.

What you'll receive

  • New sail announcements: at most 2 per day, never between 9pm and 7am. Late-night posts wait until morning.
  • Updates for sails you've joined: time changes, cancellations and who else is going.
  • Replies to your own texts, like confirmations and the list of upcoming sails.

We never send advertising or marketing, and we never text people who haven't subscribed.

Who runs this

Text to Sail is operated by Text2Sail, a volunteer service for Port Townsend small-boat sailors. It's free to use. Questions? Email henryrose@gmail.com or text HELP.
